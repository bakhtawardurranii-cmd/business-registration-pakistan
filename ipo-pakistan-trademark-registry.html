Who you're really filing with

IPO-Pakistan's role, plainly

IPO-Pakistan oversees the Trademarks Registry, the Copyright Office, and the Patent Office under one federal umbrella. When you register a trademark, it's IPO-Pakistan — not SECP, and not a private registry — that examines, publishes, and ultimately issues your certificate.

We file every trademark application directly with IPO-Pakistan on your behalf, so you have a clear, traceable filing rather than one routed through an unnecessary intermediary.

Common questions

Answered

What does IPO-Pakistan do?

The Intellectual Property Organization of Pakistan (IPO-Pakistan) is the federal body responsible for administering trademark, copyright, and patent registration nationwide.

Is IPO-Pakistan the same as SECP?

No. SECP registers and regulates companies. IPO-Pakistan is a separate federal body specifically responsible for intellectual property registration, including trademarks.

Why does it matter that a consultant files directly with IPO-Pakistan?

Filing directly with the actual regulator, rather than through an intermediary who re-routes your application, reduces delays and gives you direct visibility into your application's status.
