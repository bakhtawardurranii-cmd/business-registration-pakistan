Income tax return last date in Pakistan for 2026

The FBR return due date 2026 (tax year 2026) is 30 September 2026 for salaried individuals, other individuals and AOPs. Companies with a 30 June year-end have until 31 December 2026. Treat 30 September as final, because extensions are not guaranteed.

What are the FBR return due dates for tax year 2026?

Return due dates, tax year 2026 (1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026)
TaxpayerLast date
Salaried and other individuals30 September 2026
Associations of persons (AOPs)30 September 2026
Companies with a 30 June year-end31 December 2026

These dates come from section 118 of the Income Tax Ordinance. Companies with a different year-end file within the period the law allows after that year-end.

Will FBR extend the 2026 deadline?

At the time of writing we found no notification extending it. The law lets an individual apply for an extension of up to 15 days under section 119, but only before the due date. In 2025 FBR publicly rejected reports of an extension. Plan to file by 30 September, and check FBR's website for any notification.

What happens if you miss the income tax return deadline?

Late filing can attract a penalty, and it delays your entry on the Active Taxpayer List, which means higher withholding tax on bank, property and vehicle transactions until you are back on it. Penalty amounts are set in the law and change, so we do not quote a figure here; ask before you assume. The practical advice is to file late rather than not at all. See non-filer tax rates in Pakistan.

Can you still file in the last few days?

Yes, if you have your NTN, IRIS login and documents ready. The IRIS portal is usually busiest in the final week, so start early. If you cannot log in, fix that first; see FBR IRIS login and registration help. If you have no NTN, register first; see NTN registration in Pakistan. Then follow the steps in income tax filing in Pakistan.

Roman Urdu: return ki aakhri tareekh kya hai?

Tax year 2026 ke liye individuals aur AOPs ka return 30 September 2026 tak jama hona chahiye. Companies (30 June year-end) ke liye 31 December 2026 hai.

Questions people ask about the return deadline

What is the last date to file an income tax return in Pakistan?

30 September 2026 for individuals and AOPs for tax year 2026. 31 December 2026 for companies with a 30 June year-end.

Does the deadline apply to overseas Pakistanis?

The filing obligation follows your residency status and income. See overseas Pakistani tax filing and confirm your position.

Do I need to file if I earned nothing?

You may not owe tax, but filing a nil return helps you stay on the Active Taxpayer List. Check your own situation against section 114.
