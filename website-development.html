Choosing an approach

A platform build or a custom build?

Neither is better in general. The right choice depends on what your site has to do, your budget and how quickly you need it live. We recommend one after we understand your requirements.

Common for business sites

Platform build

For example, WordPress
  • Best forCompany sites, service businesses, portfolios and blogs with standard needs.
  • EditingYou can usually update text and images yourself, without a developer.
  • Budget and timeGenerally quicker and more economical, because proven building blocks are reused.
  • LimitsVery specific features may need extra plugins or custom work.

Custom build

Written specifically for your project
  • Best forSites with unusual features, custom workflows or strict performance needs.
  • EditingContent editing is built in only where it's needed, so we plan it with you.
  • Budget and timeMore to design, build and test, so it generally takes longer and costs more.
  • LimitsChanges after launch usually go through a developer.
