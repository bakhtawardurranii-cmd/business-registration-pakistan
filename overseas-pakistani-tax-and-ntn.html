Overseas Pakistani tax filing: NTN for non-residents and what you must report

An overseas Pakistani can register an NTN on IRIS using a CNIC or NICOP and file from abroad. What you owe depends on your residency status and on Pakistani income such as rent, property gains or business profits. Foreign salary earned abroad is treated differently for non-residents.

Can an overseas Pakistani get an NTN? (NRP NTN registration)

Yes. Non-resident Pakistanis register through the same IRIS process, choosing the correct citizen category and using a valid CNIC or NICOP. See NTN registration in Pakistan for the steps.

When does Pakistan tax an overseas Pakistani?

Residency under the Income Tax Ordinance decides the scope. Non-residents are generally taxed on Pakistan-source income. Spending long periods in Pakistan can change your status, so track your days.

Why file even if you owe nothing?

Filing keeps you on the ATL, which lowers tax when you buy or sell property, use banking and register vehicles in Pakistan. See non-filer tax rates in Pakistan.

How do you file from abroad?

IRIS is online. You need a working Pakistani mobile number or a way to receive verification codes. A consultant can help if you cannot. Start with income tax filing in Pakistan.

Records overseas Pakistanis should keep

Passport and NICOP copies, dates of arrival and departure, rental agreements and bank statements for Pakistani income. These support your residency position and your return.

Property and vehicles in Pakistan

Buying or selling property in Pakistan carries advance tax that depends on filer status, so register and file first where you can: how to become a tax filer in Pakistan.

Questions people ask about overseas tax

Do I need to visit Pakistan to get an NTN?

No. Registration is online, but you need verification codes.

Is foreign salary taxable in Pakistan for non-residents?

Generally not, but confirm your residency status.

Do overseas Pakistanis have the same deadline?

Yes, the same statutory dates apply. See deadlines.
