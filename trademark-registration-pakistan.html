Common questions

Trademark registration, answered

How is trademark registration different from company registration?

Company registration is filed with SECP and creates a legal business entity. Trademark registration is filed separately with IPO-Pakistan and protects a specific brand name, logo, or slogan from being used by competitors.

How long does trademark registration take in Pakistan?

Filing itself takes a few days, but full registration typically takes 12 to 18 months because of the mandatory examination and four-month public opposition period before a certificate can be issued.

Can I register a logo and a brand name as separate trademarks?

Yes, a wordmark and a logo are typically filed as separate trademark applications, since they protect different elements of your brand.
