Trademark registration in Pakistan: protect your brand name and logo

Trademark registration in Pakistan is done with the Intellectual Property Organization of Pakistan (IPO-Pakistan) under the Trade Marks Ordinance, 2001. Your mark is examined, published for a two-month opposition period and then registered for ten years, renewable. A straightforward case commonly takes 12 to 18 months.

What does a registered trademark protect?

A registered trademark gives you the exclusive right to use your brand identifier for the goods or services in your class, and the right to stop others from using an identical or confusingly similar mark. It protects names, logos and slogans, not business registration itself; that is the separate work of SECP. See business registration in Pakistan.

Wordmark or logo: which should you register first?

A wordmark protects the name in any style. A logo protects a specific design. Many businesses file the name first and add the logo once the design is final. If the logo changes, the logo registration may need to be refiled, while the wordmark stays protected.

How long does trademark registration take and what are the stages?

Stages: filing, examination, possible objections, publication in the Trade Marks Journal, a two-month opposition period, then registration. Guides quote anything from about 6 to 24 months; 12 to 18 months is common when no one opposes. Read the detailed guide: how to register a trademark in Pakistan.

What does trademark registration cost?

Government fees are per class and are set by IPO-Pakistan. See trademark registration cost in Pakistan.

Do you need to register the company first?

You can file as an individual or as a company. Many founders incorporate first so the trademark stands in the company's name: company registration in Pakistan.

Questions people ask about trademark registration

Is trademark registration compulsory in Pakistan?

No, but a registered mark gives much stronger rights than an unregistered one.

How long does a trademark last?

Ten years from the filing date, renewable indefinitely. See renewal and enforcement.

Can I register my business name as a trademark?

If it is distinctive and not already taken in your class. Search first.
