The Real Differentiator

PSEB certification is where startup registration actually pays off.

Registering as a company gets you incorporated. PSEB certification is what turns that into a tax advantage — if you qualify.

For tech & IT exporters

PSEB-Certified Startup

Real tax benefits
  • Income tax Exemption for the certification year plus the following two tax years, under Section 143 of the Second Schedule
  • Export income Reduced withholding — 0.25% instead of the standard 1% on IT export remittances
  • Requirements Active NTN, filer status, a genuine physical office, and an IT/IT-enabled services focus

Standard Company Registration

No automatic exemptions
  • Income tax Standard corporate tax rates apply — no exemption without PSEB certification
  • Export income Standard 1% withholding on export remittances applies
  • Best for Non-tech businesses, or startups not yet ready for PSEB certification

PSEB Eligibility

What qualifies your startup.

✓
Legally registered business — SECP company, partnership, or sole proprietorship all qualify
✓
Active NTN and current status as a tax filer
✓
A genuine physical office address — virtual/co-working addresses without proper documentation won't qualify
✓
Primary business focus on IT or IT-enabled services (software development, web services, and similar)
