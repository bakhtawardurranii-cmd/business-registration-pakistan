Non-filer tax rates in Pakistan: where you pay more, and how to stop

Non-filers in Pakistan pay higher withholding tax than filers on bank profit, cash withdrawals, vehicle registration and property transactions. Rates change with each Finance Act, and published figures disagree, so check FBR's current withholding rate card before any big transaction.

Where do non-filers pay more tax?

We do not print percentage figures because they change with each budget and sources disagree. Ask for the current rate from FBR or your bank before you transact.

Advance tax on vehicles in Pakistan

Advance tax is collected when a vehicle is registered or transferred and depends on engine size and on whether the buyer is on the ATL. Being a filer normally lowers it. Check the current schedule with the excise office before purchase.

Capital gain tax on property in Pakistan

Tax on a property sale depends on the holding period, the property type and the seller's filer status. Buyers also pay advance tax at registration, again at different rates for filers and non-filers. The rules changed in recent Finance Acts, so property deals are where professional advice is most valuable. A property tax consultant in Pakistan can calculate both sides before you sign.

How do you stop paying non-filer rates?

Register and file: how to become a tax filer in Pakistan. Confirm the result: Active Taxpayer List (ATL) check by CNIC. Do this before the transaction, not after, because withholding is deducted at the time of the transaction.

Questions people ask about non-filer rates

Are non-filer tax rates the same for everyone?

No. They depend on the type of transaction, and for property, vehicles and the holding period.

Can I claim back the extra tax I paid as a non-filer?

Excess tax withheld can be adjusted in your return, subject to the law. Keep every deduction receipt and see the filing guide.

Should I check the rate before buying property?

Yes. The difference between filer and non-filer rates on property can be large. Check first, or get advice.
