Logo trademark vs wordmark: which protects more?

A wordmark protects your brand name in plain text, regardless of styling. A logo trademark protects your visual design specifically. Most brands eventually register both, but a wordmark is usually the higher-priority first filing.

Common questions

Answered

Should I register my brand name or my logo first?

Most businesses register the wordmark (brand name) first, since it protects the name regardless of how it's styled, then add the logo as a second application once the visual identity is finalized.

Can I register both a wordmark and a logo?

Yes, and many established brands do — a wordmark registration and a logo registration are filed as separate applications, each protecting a different element of your brand.

If I register my logo, does that protect the words inside it?

Not necessarily. A logo (device mark) registration can be narrower than a wordmark if the text within it isn't the dominant, clearly readable element — a separate wordmark filing is the safer way to protect the name itself.
