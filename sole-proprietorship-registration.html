Sole proprietorship registration: the simplest way to start.

Sole proprietorship isn’t registered with SECP at all — it’s simply operating a business under your own name or a trade name, registered directly with FBR for an NTN, with no separate legal entity involved.
Register your NTN Compare with SMC

How it works

No SECP filing, just an NTN

A sole proprietorship is the default way to run a business alone in Pakistan — you register directly with FBR for an NTN tied to your personal CNIC, and there's no separate company to incorporate. It's the fastest and cheapest way to start operating legally.

The trade-off is liability: since there's no legal separation between you and the business, you're personally on the hook for any business debts. If that's a concern, an SMC gives you similar simplicity with limited liability instead.

Common questions

Answered

Does a sole proprietorship need to register with SECP?

No. A sole proprietorship never registers with SECP. It's simply you operating a business under your own name or a trade name, registered directly with FBR for an NTN.

Am I personally liable for business debts as a sole proprietor?

Yes. There's no legal separation between you and the business, so your personal assets can be used to satisfy business debts.

Can I later convert a sole proprietorship into an SMC or private limited company?

Yes, many founders start as a sole proprietor and later incorporate as an SMC or private limited company as the business grows — this is a fresh SECP registration, not a conversion of the old FBR registration.
