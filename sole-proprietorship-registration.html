Sole proprietorship registration in Pakistan: the NTN-only route

A sole proprietorship in Pakistan is not registered with SECP. You operate under your own name or a trade name and register for an NTN with FBR. It is the simplest start, but you are personally liable for all business debts.

How do you register a sole proprietorship?

Get your NTN on IRIS (NTN registration in Pakistan), add your business activity, and open a bank account in your name or trade name. Register for sales tax only if your activity requires it: sales tax registration in Pakistan (STRN) or provincial sales tax on services (PRA, SRB, KPRA, BRA).

How does a sole proprietor file taxes?

Business income goes on your personal return: income tax filing in Pakistan.

When should you switch to an SMC?

When liability, larger contracts or credibility matter. See SMC (single member company) registration in Pakistan.

Records a sole proprietor should keep

Keep sales and purchase invoices, bank statements, rent and utility bills for the business, and a simple ledger. These support your return and answer any FBR query. See FBR notice reply and tax audit support for what a query looks like.

Questions people ask about a sole proprietorship

Do I need SECP for a sole proprietorship?

No.

Can I use a trade name?

Yes, but check that it does not infringe another business's trademark.

Is a sole proprietorship a company?

No. There is no separate legal entity.
