SMC registration in Pakistan: a company for one owner

A single member company (SMC) is a private company with exactly one member. It gives a solo founder limited liability. SECP requires a nominee, who takes over the shares if the sole member dies. The nominee's details are filed with the incorporation papers.

Why choose an SMC instead of a sole proprietorship?

A sole proprietor is personally liable for business debts. An SMC is a separate legal person, so your liability is limited to the capital you invested. That matters for contracts, banking and credibility. Compare all options at business registration in Pakistan.

What is the SMC nominee requirement?

You name a nominee in the incorporation documents. The nominee is not an owner today; they would take over your shares on your death. You can change the nominee later by filing with SECP.

What do you need to register an SMC?

Your CNIC, nominee details, a company name, the MOA and AOA and a registered address. See documents required for company registration. The sole member usually also acts as director.

Can you convert an SMC later?

Yes. If you add a co-founder you can convert to a private limited company. The Companies Act, 2017 allows conversion in both directions.

Questions people ask about an SMC

Is SMC the same as a private limited company?

An SMC is a type of private company with one member.

Can a freelancer register an SMC?

Yes. See freelancer tax filing for tax considerations.

Is the nominee a director?

The nominee takes over the shares on the member's death. It is not the same as being a working director.
