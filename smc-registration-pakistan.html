SMC registration: limited liability for solo founders.

An SMC (Single Member Company) lets one person own 100% of a limited liability company without needing a co-founder — the most common structure solo founders in Pakistan choose over a sole proprietorship.
Start your registration Compare with private limited

Why founders choose SMC

Liability protection without a co-founder

A sole proprietorship is simpler to start, but leaves you personally liable for business debts. An SMC gives you the same limited liability as a private limited company — your personal assets stay protected — while letting you remain the sole owner.

SECP does require you to name a nominee director, who has no ownership stake but would step in only if something happened to you. Beyond that, you retain full control of the business.

Common questions

Answered

Do I need a co-founder to register an SMC?

No. An SMC (Single Member Company) is specifically designed for one person to own 100% of a limited liability company without a co-founder.

What is a nominee director in an SMC?

SECP requires an SMC to name a nominee director who would take over management in the event the sole member dies or becomes incapacitated — the nominee doesn't hold shares.

Can I convert my SMC into a private limited company later?

Yes. If you bring in a co-founder or investor later, SECP allows you to convert an SMC into a private limited company through a separate filing.
