How to register a company in Pakistan: the complete guide.

Registering a company in Pakistan means choosing a structure, reserving a name with SECP, filing incorporation documents, and then registering separately with FBR for an NTN. Most founders complete SECP incorporation in 3–7 working days.

Common questions

Company registration, step by step

What's the very first step to register a company in Pakistan?

Deciding which company type fits your situation, since that determines which forms and documents you'll file. Most solo founders choose an SMC; multi-owner businesses choose a private limited company.

Can I register a company online without visiting SECP in person?

Yes. SECP's eZfile and LEAP portals allow the entire incorporation process to be completed online, without visiting a physical SECP office.
