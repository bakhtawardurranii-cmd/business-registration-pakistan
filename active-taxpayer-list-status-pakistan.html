Are you on FBR's Active Taxpayer List?

The Active Taxpayer List (ATL) is FBR’s list of taxpayers who filed their return on time. Being on it lowers the withholding tax rate you pay on many transactions, while being off it raises that rate significantly.
Check your status File your return

Why it matters

ATL status affects your withholding tax rate

FBR applies a lower withholding tax rate to "active" filers on the ATL, and a substantially higher rate to those who aren't — on things like bank cash withdrawals, property transactions, vehicle registration, and profit on savings. For a business or high-transaction individual, this difference adds up quickly.

Your name is added to the ATL after you file your return for the relevant tax year, and it updates periodically. Filing a return late can mean a delay before you're added back, so timely filing matters even beyond the tax you actually owe.

Common questions

Answered

What is the Active Taxpayer List (ATL)?

The ATL is FBR's published list of taxpayers who filed their income tax return for the most recent tax year by the deadline (or a later date FBR notifies).

How do I check if I'm on the ATL?

You can check your ATL status on FBR's website using your CNIC (for individuals) or your company's NTN, or ask us to confirm it for you.

What happens if I'm not on the ATL?

Being off the ATL means a significantly higher withholding tax rate applies to many of your transactions, such as bank withdrawals, property purchases, and vehicle registration, compared to being an active filer.
