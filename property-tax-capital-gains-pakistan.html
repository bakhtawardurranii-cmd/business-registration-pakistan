Property tax in Pakistan: what the buyer and the seller each pay

When a property is sold, up to three taxes can apply: advance tax from the seller (section 236C), advance tax from the buyer (section 236K), and capital gains tax on the seller's profit (section 37). For tax year 2027, filers pay 2.75% as seller and 1.25% as buyer, and property bought from 1 July 2024 is taxed at a flat 15% on the gain. Non-filers pay much more.

These rates come from the Finance Act 2026 as reported by professional tax summaries. A budget speech and the final Act do not always match, so confirm on FBR's rate card before you sign. Provincial costs such as stamp duty and CVT are separate and are not covered here.

A worked example for a seller

Suppose you bought a house in 2025 for Rs 6,000,000 and sell it for Rs 10,000,000, and you are on the ATL. Advance tax under 236C is 2.75% of Rs 10,000,000, which is Rs 275,000. Capital gains tax is 15% of the gain of Rs 4,000,000, which is Rs 600,000. Advance tax is generally adjustable against your final tax for the year, so the extra you would pay at filing is roughly Rs 325,000.

This is a simple illustration. Real cases depend on documented purchase costs, the dates, and what counts as a cost. Do not use it for a sale deed without checking the numbers.

A worked example for a buyer

If the fair market value of the property is Rs 10,000,000 and you are a filer, 236K is 1.25%, which is Rs 125,000. This is paid at the time the transfer is registered, so it has to be ready before you go to the registrar, not after.

What changes if you are a non-filer?

Advance tax under 236C and 236K is several times higher, and capital gains tax is charged at normal slab rates with a floor of 15%. On the same sale, the total cash cost for a non-filer can be close to double. If you are about to buy or sell, check both parties with the ATL check first, and read filer vs non-filer if you need to fix your status before the deal.

Overseas Pakistanis with a POC or NICOP who are non-resident can use the filer rate under FBR's published procedure, even without being on the ATL. See overseas Pakistani tax filing.

Before the registrar: a short checklist

  • Check the ATL status of the buyer and the seller.
  • Confirm the fair market value FBR uses for the property.
  • Work out 236C, 236K and any capital gains tax before the day.
  • Keep proof of the purchase price and costs for the seller's return.
  • Report the sale in your next return and, if it applies, your wealth statement.

Questions people ask about property tax and capital gains

What is section 236C?

Advance income tax collected from the seller when immovable property is sold. For filers in tax year 2027 it is 2.75% of the amount received.

What is section 236K?

Advance income tax collected from the buyer when property is bought. For filers in tax year 2027 it is 1.25% of the fair market value.

Is capital gains tax on property 15%?

For property bought from 1 July 2024, filers pay a flat 15% on the gain. Older purchases follow the holding period table. Non-filers pay normal slab rates with a minimum of 15%.

Can I adjust the advance tax I paid?

Property advance tax is generally adjustable against your final tax for the year, so it is credited in your return. Keep the payment records.

Does the buyer or the seller pay stamp duty?

Stamp duty and CVT are provincial charges and depend on the province and the transaction. They are separate from the federal taxes on this page.
