Foreign company registration in Pakistan: what overseas founders need

Foreign nationals and overseas Pakistanis can incorporate a company in Pakistan through SECP using the same process as residents. Differences are mainly documents, such as passport copies, and sector rules on foreign ownership. A private company generally needs at least two directors, so plan for that.

Can a foreigner register a company in Pakistan?

Yes. Follow how to register a company in Pakistan. Foreign directors and members provide passport copies in place of CNICs. Check whether your sector limits foreign ownership before you file.

A foreign company with a place of business in Pakistan

A company incorporated abroad that establishes a place of business in Pakistan registers under a separate part of the Companies Act, with its own SECP fee category. Ask for advice on which route applies.

Banking and tax after registration

Open a company bank account and register with FBR: FBR registration after SECP incorporation. Overseas founders should also read overseas Pakistani tax filing and NRP NTN.

A practical checklist for overseas founders

  • Confirm your sector's rules on foreign ownership.
  • Arrange attested passport copies and a Pakistani registered address.
  • Plan how the company bank account will be opened and funded.
  • Appoint someone in Pakistan to receive official notices.
  • Register for tax immediately after incorporation: FBR registration after SECP incorporation.

Questions people ask about foreign company registration

Do I need to be in Pakistan to incorporate?

Filing is online, but signatures and document attestation may need arranging.

Is a Pakistani director required?

Requirements can apply to at least one director. Confirm for your structure.

Which structure suits a foreign founder?

A private limited company is common. See private limited.
