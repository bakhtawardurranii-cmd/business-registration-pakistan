Registering a company in Pakistan as a foreign founder.

Foreign nationals and overseas Pakistanis can register a company in Pakistan through the same SECP process as resident founders, though foreign shareholding in certain sectors may also require filing with the Board of Investment (BOI).
Start your registration See the general process

What's different

Extra steps for foreign founders

The core SECP incorporation process — name reservation, MOA/AOA, and filing — is the same regardless of nationality. The main difference is documentation: foreign directors typically submit an attested passport copy instead of a CNIC, and some sectors require Board of Investment registration alongside SECP incorporation.

Repatriating profits and opening a corporate bank account as a foreign-owned entity can also involve additional State Bank of Pakistan documentation, which we help coordinate alongside the registration itself.

Common questions

Answered

Can a foreign national own 100% of a Pakistani company?

In most sectors, yes — Pakistan allows full foreign ownership, though certain sectors have specific foreign investment restrictions or require additional approval.

Is BOI registration required for every foreign-owned company?

Not always. Board of Investment registration is typically required for specific incentive schemes or sectors, rather than universally for all foreign shareholding.

Does a foreign director need to be physically present in Pakistan to register a company?

No, incorporation documents can generally be signed and submitted remotely with properly attested and legalized copies, without the director needing to travel to Pakistan.
