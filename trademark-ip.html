Common confusion

Trademark, copyright, or business name — what actually protects your brand?

These three get mixed up constantly. Here's what each one actually does.

What most brands need

Trademark

Protects your brand identity
  • Covers Your brand name, logo, or slogan as a marketplace identifier
  • Filed with IPO-Pakistan's Trade Marks Registry, under the Trade Marks Ordinance 2001
  • Duration 10 years, renewable indefinitely
  • Stops Others from using a confusingly similar mark on similar goods/services

Copyright

Protects creative work
  • Covers Original written, visual, or artistic work — a logo's artwork, website copy, designs
  • Filed with Separate copyright registration, distinct from trademark filing
  • Duration Generally the creator's lifetime plus a set period after
  • Stops Others from copying the actual creative work itself

Business Name Registration

Not the same as a trademark
  • Covers Your legal right to operate under that name with SECP/FBR
  • Filed with SECP (company) or FBR (sole proprietorship) — not IPO-Pakistan
  • Duration Tied to your business registration, not 10-year renewal cycles
  • Stops Nothing on its own — it doesn't prevent others from trademarking a similar brand name
How It Works

From search to certificate.

Trademark registration in Pakistan moves through a fixed legal sequence — we manage each stage and keep you updated.

1

Search & classify

We search the Trade Marks Register for conflicts and classify your goods/services under the Nice Classification.

2

File with IPO-Pakistan

Your application is submitted to the Trade Marks Registry with all required specimens and documentation.

3

Examination & publication

The examiner reviews your mark; if accepted, it's published in the Trade Marks Journal for a 2-month opposition window.

4

Registration certificate

If no opposition is filed, IPO-Pakistan issues your certificate — valid for 10 years from application.

Pakistan joined the Madrid Protocol in 2021, so a Pakistani applicant can file a single international application through IPO-Pakistan's Trade Marks Registry (as the Office of Origin) and extend protection to other Madrid System member countries. You can also file a standalone national application directly with IPO-Pakistan if you only need protection within Pakistan.

After your mark is published in the Trade Marks Journal, third parties have a 2-month window to file an opposition. If that happens, we help you respond within the required timeline — this is a formal legal process, so timely response matters.
