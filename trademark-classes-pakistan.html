Trademark classes in Pakistan, explained.

Pakistan uses the international Nice Classification’s 45 classes (34 for goods, 11 for services) to categorize trademarks. You must file in the class matching your actual goods or services, and Pakistan doesn’t allow multi-class filing in one application.

Common questions

Answered

How many trademark classes are there in Pakistan?

Pakistan uses the international Nice Classification system with 45 classes total — 34 for goods and 11 for services.

Can I file one trademark in multiple classes at once?

No. Pakistan requires a separate application for each class, unlike some countries that allow multi-class filing in a single application.

What happens if I choose the wrong class?

If your registered class doesn't match your actual goods or services, your trademark won't legally protect your real business activity, and you may need to file again in the correct class.
