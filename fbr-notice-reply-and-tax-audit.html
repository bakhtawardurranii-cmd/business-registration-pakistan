FBR notice reply services: what to do in the first 48 hours after a notice

If you receive an FBR notice, do not ignore it. Read the section cited, note the reply date, gather the documents it asks for and reply through IRIS or as the notice directs. Missing the date can lead to an assessment against you, so seek help early if the notice is unclear.

What should you do first when you get an FBR notice?

  1. Note the date of the notice and the reply date.
  2. Identify the law section and the tax year it refers to.
  3. Check whether it is an information request, a query on your return, or an audit selection.
  4. Collect bank statements, invoices, contracts and wealth statement records for that year.
  5. Reply in writing with attachments. Keep proof of submission.

Common reasons FBR sends notices

  • Income or assets that do not match what was declared.
  • Withholding statements that do not agree with the return.
  • Sales tax invoices that do not match suppliers' filings.
  • Selection for audit.

Tax audit services in Pakistan: what does representation involve?

A representative reviews the notice, prepares a reply with evidence, attends hearings and requests time where needed. Fees depend on the case, so agree scope first.

Can you get more time to reply?

You can usually request a short extension in writing before the date passes. Do not wait until the last day.

How do you avoid notices in future?

File on time, keep your wealth statement reconciled and keep invoices. See income tax filing in Pakistan.

Questions people ask about an FBR notice

What happens if I do not reply to an FBR notice?

FBR can proceed to an assessment based on its information, which may increase your liability.

Is an FBR notice always a penalty?

No. Many notices ask for information or clarification.
