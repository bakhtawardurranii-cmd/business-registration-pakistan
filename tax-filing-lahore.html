Common questions

Answered

Do I file taxes at a specific FBR office in Lahore?

Filing itself happens through FBR's national IRIS portal, the same system used across Pakistan. Our Lahore team handles document collection and submission on your behalf, but there's no separate 'Lahore filing system.'

Can I get help with both NTN registration and annual filing in one place?

Yes — we handle NTN registration, sales tax registration, and annual return filing together, so you don't need to coordinate multiple people.
