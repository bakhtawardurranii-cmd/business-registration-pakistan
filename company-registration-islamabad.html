Company registration in Islamabad, filed with SECP.

Company registration in Islamabad is filed with SECP's head office, which covers Islamabad and Rawalpindi. We handle name reservation, document preparation, and SECP filing, plus local registrations like the Islamabad Chamber of Commerce & Industry (ICCI) where relevant.

Local filing, same national process

What's different about registering in Islamabad

The SECP incorporation process itself is identical nationwide — name reservation, MOA/AOA, and the eZfile/LEAP portal all work the same way regardless of city. What differs is which SECP office reviews your filing, and which local bodies you may also need to register with.

In Islamabad, that typically means the Islamabad Chamber of Commerce & Industry (ICCI), depending on your business activity and whether you have employees. We handle the SECP filing directly and can advise on which local registrations actually apply to you.
