Partnership firm registration in Pakistan.

A partnership firm is registered with the Registrar of Firms, not SECP, letting two or more people share ownership and profits without issuing shares — a simpler middle ground between a sole proprietorship and a company.
Start your registration Compare with LLP

How it works

Shared ownership, no shares

Two or more partners agree to run a business together and share profits according to a partnership deed. Registration is with the Registrar of Firms rather than SECP, which makes it quicker and less paperwork-heavy than incorporating a company.

The trade-off is liability — partners are personally responsible for the firm's debts, with no limit. If you want shared ownership with liability protection instead, an LLP is usually the better fit.

Common questions

Answered

Is a partnership firm registered with SECP?

No. A partnership firm is registered with the Registrar of Firms under the Partnership Act, not with SECP, and doesn't issue shares.

What is a partnership deed?

A partnership deed is the written agreement between partners setting out profit-sharing ratios, responsibilities, and what happens if a partner leaves — it's required to register the firm.

Are partners personally liable for the firm's debts?

Yes, in a standard partnership firm, partners have unlimited personal liability for the firm's debts, unlike an LLP where liability is limited.
