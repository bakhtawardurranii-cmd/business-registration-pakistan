Partnership firm registration in Pakistan: deed, registrar and NTN

A partnership firm is formed by a written partnership deed between two or more partners and registered with the Registrar of Firms, not SECP. Partners are personally liable for the firm's debts. For tax, the firm is an association of persons (AOP) and needs its own NTN.

How do you register a partnership firm?

  1. Draft a partnership deed covering capital, profit sharing, roles and exit.
  2. Sign it by all partners.
  3. Apply to the Registrar of Firms with the deed and partners' details.
  4. Register the firm as an AOP with FBR: NTN registration in Pakistan.

Tax treatment of a partnership

An AOP files its own return and must file regardless of income. See income tax filing in Pakistan.

Is a partnership or an LLP better?

A partnership is simple but carries unlimited liability. An LLP limits liability.

What should a partnership deed cover?

  • Names, addresses and capital of each partner.
  • Profit and loss sharing ratios.
  • Roles, decision rights and signing authority.
  • Admission, retirement and death of a partner.
  • How the firm is dissolved and assets divided.

Why joint liability deserves a second look

In an ordinary partnership each partner can be pursued for the firm's debts, including debts created by another partner acting for the firm. If that risk worries you, consider an LLP or a private limited company.

Questions people ask about a partnership firm

Is a partnership firm registered with SECP?

No. It registers with the Registrar of Firms.

Is a partnership deed compulsory?

A written deed is the basis of registration and protects all partners.

Do partnerships file tax returns?

Yes, as an AOP.
