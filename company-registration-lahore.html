Choosing Your Structure

Private Limited Company or Single Member Company?

Both are registered with SECP under the Companies Act 2017 — the right one depends on how many owners your business has. Same process, whether you file from Lahore or anywhere else in Pakistan.

Most common

Private Limited Company

Two or more owners
  • Directors Minimum 2 directors/members required by law
  • Capital Minimum share capital of PKR 100,000
  • Liability Limited to shareholding — personal assets protected
  • Best for Co-founders, family businesses, or any company with multiple owners

Single Member Company (SMC)

One owner, still incorporated
  • Directors Just 1 director required — you can incorporate alone
  • Capital Minimum share capital of PKR 100,000
  • Liability Same limited liability protection as a private limited company
  • Best for Solo founders who want corporate structure without a co-owner
