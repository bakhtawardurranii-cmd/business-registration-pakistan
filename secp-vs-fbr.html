SECP vs FBR registration: which one do you need first?

SECP registers companies and gives them legal existence. FBR registers taxpayers and issues the NTN used for tax. A company registers with SECP first, then with FBR. A sole proprietor registers only with FBR. Each registration does a different job, and neither replaces the other.

What is the difference between SECP and FBR?

SECP compared with FBR
SECPFBR
Full nameSecurities and Exchange Commission of PakistanFederal Board of Revenue
Main jobRegisters and regulates companiesCollects federal taxes
You receiveCertificate of incorporationNTN, sales tax number, filer status
Who registersCompanies, LLPsAll taxpayers, including companies

Which do you register with first?

Companies: SECP first, then FBR. See FBR registration after SECP incorporation. Sole proprietors and freelancers: FBR only. See sole proprietorship registration in Pakistan and NTN registration in Pakistan.

Do you need both?

A company needs both to operate properly. SECP alone leaves you unable to file taxes, and FBR alone does not create a company.

Where to go next

Common mix-ups between SECP and FBR

  • Believing the SECP certificate lets you invoice. You need an NTN first.
  • Believing an NTN makes you a company. It does not.
  • Believing sole proprietors need SECP. They do not.
  • Confusing filer status, which is an FBR matter, with company status: Active Taxpayer List (ATL) check by CNIC.

Questions people ask about SECP and FBR

Can I have an NTN without SECP registration?

Yes. Individuals and sole proprietors do.

Does SECP registration give me an NTN automatically?

No. You register with FBR separately.

Is a partnership registered with SECP?

A partnership firm registers with the Registrar of Firms. An LLP registers with SECP. See LLP and partnership.
