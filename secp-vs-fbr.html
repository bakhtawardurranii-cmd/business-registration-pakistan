How the two connect

If you're incorporating a company

Register with SECP first to get your certificate of incorporation. Only after that can you register your company with FBR for an NTN — you can't get a company NTN without an incorporated company behind it.

Common questions

SECP vs FBR, answered

What's the difference between SECP and FBR?

SECP regulates and registers companies, giving them legal existence. FBR handles taxation — NTN registration, sales tax, and annual tax returns — for both companies and individuals.

Do I register with SECP or FBR first?

If you're incorporating a company, SECP comes first — you need your certificate of incorporation before FBR will issue a company NTN. Sole proprietors skip SECP entirely and register directly with FBR.

Can a company be registered with FBR but not SECP?

No. A company, by definition, is incorporated through SECP. A business without SECP registration operating under FBR alone is legally a sole proprietorship, not a company.
