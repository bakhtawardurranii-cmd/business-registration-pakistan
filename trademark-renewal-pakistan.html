Don't let your trademark protection lapse.

Trademarks registered in Pakistan must be renewed every 10 years with IPO-Pakistan. Missing the renewal window risks losing protection entirely, potentially letting someone else register your mark.

Staying protected

What the renewal process involves

Renewal is far simpler than the original registration — there's no new examination or opposition period, just a filing confirming you want to keep the mark active, along with the renewal fee.

We track your trademark's renewal date and reach out well before it's due, so you're never at risk of losing protection because a deadline slipped past unnoticed.

Common questions

Answered

How often do I need to renew a trademark in Pakistan?

Every 10 years from the date of registration, indefinitely — a trademark can be renewed repeatedly as long as it's kept up to date.

What happens if I miss my trademark renewal deadline?

IPO-Pakistan typically allows a grace period after expiry, but beyond that window the trademark can lapse entirely, meaning someone else could register the same mark.

Does renewal require re-filing the whole application?

No, renewal is a simpler filing than the original application — you're not re-examined from scratch, just confirming continued registration.
