Trademark registration fees in Pakistan: what you pay per class

Recent guides list IPO-Pakistan's filing fee at Rs 3,000 per class (Form TM-1) and the registration certificate fee at Rs 9,000 per class (Form TM-11). Renewal is reported at about Rs 15,000. Fees can change, so confirm with IPO-Pakistan's fee and forms page before you pay.

How much are IPO-Pakistan's trademark fees?

Government fees per class (as reported in recent guides)
StageFormFee
Filing an applicationTM-1Rs 3,000
Registration certificateTM-11Rs 9,000
Renewal (every ten years; see renewal rules)TM-12About Rs 15,000 (estimate)

We have not verified these figures against IPO-Pakistan's current published schedule, so treat them as a guide and check the official fee and forms page before paying.

Why does the total cost vary?

  • Each application covers one class. Two classes mean two applications and two sets of fees.
  • A name and a logo are separate applications.
  • Replying to objections or an opposition can add professional fees.

How to keep trademark fees down

Register only the classes you use now. Search first so you do not pay for an application that will be refused. See trademark classes in Pakistan (Nice classification) and how to register a trademark in Pakistan.

Worked example: government fees for one and two classes

Using the reported figures, one class costs Rs 3,000 to file and Rs 9,000 to register, so Rs 12,000 in government fees. Two classes cost Rs 24,000. Professional fees and renewal are extra. Confirm current fees with IPO-Pakistan before budgeting.

Questions people ask about trademark fees

Do I pay the registration fee at the start?

No. The filing fee comes first. The registration fee is requested after acceptance.

Is there a government fee to search?

Ask IPO-Pakistan; search arrangements and fees can change.

Do consultants charge extra?

Yes. Professional fees are separate from government fees.
