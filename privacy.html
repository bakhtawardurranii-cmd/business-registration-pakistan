Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we collect through this website and how it's used. Because our services involve handling CNIC, tax, and other personal documents, we've kept this deliberately plain rather than boilerplate legal language.
Questions about this policy? Back to homepage

Note to the site owner: this is a plain-language starting draft, not a legal document. Because your service handles CNIC copies and other personal/financial data, have this reviewed against Pakistan's current data-protection rules before publishing.

What we collect

When you fill out our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and whatever details you share about the service you need. If you engage us as a client, we additionally collect the documents required for your specific filing — CNIC copies, business details, and similar.

How we use it

Contact form submissions are used solely to respond to your enquiry. Documents provided as part of an engagement are used solely to complete the registration or filing you've asked us to handle, and are shared only with the relevant regulator (SECP, IPO-Pakistan, or FBR) as required to complete that filing.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information to third parties. Information is shared only where necessary to complete your filing (with the applicable government regulator) or where required by law.

How long we keep it

We retain client documents for as long as reasonably necessary to complete your engagement and to meet any recordkeeping obligations that apply to our services, after which they're securely deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete your information, can be sent through our contact page.
