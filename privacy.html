Privacy Policy

How Business Registration Pakistan collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Business Registration Pakistan handles information collected through this website. The website provides information about business registration, trademark and intellectual-property support, tax and compliance services, and digital and IT services in Pakistan. It applies to information you provide when you contact us through the website and to certain information collected automatically when you use the site.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our contact form, we may collect the information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, and the contents of your message. We may also collect certain technical and usage information automatically, such as pages viewed, approximate usage patterns, browser or device information, and similar data through analytics tools.

3. Analytics & Tracking Tools

This website uses Google Analytics (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the website and to help us improve its content, usability and performance. These services use cookies and related technologies to collect usage information. Their handling of information is also governed by their own privacy terms and policies.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use information you submit to respond to inquiries, understand what service you are asking about, communicate with you about your request, and improve our website and services. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by a website on your device. Business Registration Pakistan uses cookies and similar technologies, including those associated with analytics, to understand website usage and improve the visitor experience. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect certain website functions or the accuracy of analytics.

6. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services including Google Analytics (GA4) and Microsoft Clarity as analytics and website-improvement tools. These providers may process information collected through their services according to their own policies. See [link to Google Privacy Policy] and [link to Microsoft Privacy Policy] for their respective privacy information. These links should be replaced with the final approved URLs before publication.

7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to service providers who help us operate, maintain or improve the website, or where disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, legal process or a lawful request from an authority.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect information handled through the website. However, no website, transmission method or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to personal information you have submitted to us or ask us to delete it. You can make such a request using the contact details provided below. We may need to verify the request before taking action.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through the website.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, our practices or applicable requirements. When we make changes, the “Last updated” date above will be updated to reflect the revision.

12. Contact Us

If you have a privacy question, want to request access to or deletion of information you submitted, or need clarification about this policy, please contact us through our Contact page or email hello@businessregistrationpakistan.com.
