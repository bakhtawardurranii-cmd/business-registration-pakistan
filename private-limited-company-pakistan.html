Private limited company registration in Pakistan: rules and requirements

A private limited company in Pakistan is registered with SECP under the Companies Act, 2017. It needs at least two members and two directors, has limited liability, restricts share transfers and cannot invite the public to subscribe. Most co-founded startups and family businesses choose it.

What are the requirements for a private limited company?

  • At least two members, up to fifty (not counting employees).
  • At least two directors.
  • Memorandum and articles of association.
  • A registered office in Pakistan.
  • A reserved company name.

Private limited or SMC: which fits you?

Choose private limited if you have or expect co-owners or investors. Choose an SMC if you are the only owner. You can convert between the two later.

What are the benefits?

Limited liability, a separate legal identity, easier investment and clearer credibility with banks and larger customers.

Directors and members: what is the difference?

Members (shareholders) own the company. Directors manage it. In a small company the same people are usually both, but the roles are legally separate. Members vote on major decisions such as changing the articles. Directors run day-to-day affairs and sign filings.

What compliance follows registration?

A registered company must keep statutory records, file annual returns and financial statements with SECP, and file its own income tax return with FBR. Set up tax first: FBR registration after SECP incorporation and corporate tax consultant services in Pakistan.

Questions people ask about a private limited company

Can one person own a private limited company?

Not as an ordinary private company. One person can form a single member company (SMC).

Can foreigners be directors?

Yes. See foreign company registration.

Does it need an audit?

Audit requirements depend on the company's size and category under the Companies Act. Ask for advice on your case.
