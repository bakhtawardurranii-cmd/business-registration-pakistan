Private Limited Company registration in Pakistan.

A private limited company is a SECP-registered entity owned by two or more shareholders with limited liability — the standard structure for co-founded businesses and any startup planning to raise investment.
Start your registration Compare with SMC

Key features

What makes it a private limited company

Ownership is split between two or more shareholders, each holding shares proportional to their investment. The company is a separate legal entity, so shareholders are only liable up to the value of their shares — personal assets stay protected if the business runs into debt.

This structure is what most investors expect before they'll issue a term sheet, since shares can be transferred, diluted, or vested over time. If you're a solo founder without a co-founder yet, an SMC gives you the same liability protection without needing a second shareholder.

Common questions

Answered

What's the minimum number of shareholders for a private limited company?

SECP requires a minimum of two shareholders for a private limited company, up to a maximum of fifty.

Can a foreigner be a director of a private limited company in Pakistan?

Yes, foreign nationals can be directors and shareholders, though at least one director is generally expected to be a resident of Pakistan.

Can I convert my private limited company into an SMC later, or vice versa?

Yes, SECP allows conversion between a private limited company and an SMC as your ownership structure changes, through a separate filing.
