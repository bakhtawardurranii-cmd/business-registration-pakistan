Salary tax calculator in Pakistan: how the result is worked out

The calculator converts your salary to an annual figure, finds the slab it falls in, and applies the fixed amount plus the percentage on the excess. Example for tax year 2027: a taxable salary of Rs 150,000 a month is Rs 1,800,000 a year. Tax is Rs 6,000 + 11% of Rs 600,000, which is Rs 72,000 a year, or Rs 6,000 a month.

What to do after you know your tax

If you earn more than the exempt threshold, you need an NTN and a return; see income tax filing in Pakistan and NTN registration in Pakistan. If your employer already deducts tax, filing a return still reconciles it. Income beyond salary, such as rent or freelance work, is not covered by this calculator. See freelancer and IT exporter tax filing in Pakistan.

Questions people ask about the tax calculator

How much tax do I pay on a Rs 100,000 monthly salary in Pakistan?

Under the tax year 2027 slabs, Rs 100,000 a month is Rs 1,200,000 a year. The tax is 1% of the amount over Rs 600,000, which is Rs 6,000 a year, or Rs 500 a month.

Is this the same as the salary tax calculator on FBR's website?

It uses the same published slab rates but is not FBR's tool. Use it for planning and confirm your final figure on IRIS.

Does the calculator work for business owners and freelancers?

No. Non-salaried individuals are taxed on a different slab table. Ask us for a review of your business or freelance income.

Which tax year should I choose?

Choose tax year 2027 for salary earned from July 2026. Choose tax year 2026 for the return being filed now, which covers July 2025 to June 2026.
