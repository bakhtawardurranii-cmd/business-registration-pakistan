Tax filing services in Pakistan: what you need and who can do it

Tax filing services in Pakistan cover NTN registration, annual income tax returns, filer (ATL) status, sales tax and replies to FBR notices. Individuals can file free on the IRIS portal. A consultant is worth hiring when your income, assets, business or a notice makes the return hard to get right.

Which tax service do you actually need?

Most people arrive with one of five problems. Match yours to the right guide:

  • I have no NTN yet. Start with NTN registration in Pakistan.
  • I need to file my yearly return. See income tax filing in Pakistan and the income tax return last date in Pakistan.
  • I am a non-filer paying higher tax. Read how to become a tax filer in Pakistan.
  • My business sells goods or services. You may need sales tax registration in Pakistan (STRN) or provincial sales tax on services (PRA, SRB, KPRA, BRA).
  • I received an FBR notice. Go to FBR notice reply and tax audit support before the reply date passes.

Can you file your own return, or should you hire a tax consultant?

You can file your own return. FBR does not charge for registration or for filing on IRIS. Many salaried people with one employer and no other income do this every year.

When you hire a tax consultant in Pakistan it should be because one of these applies: you have business or rental income, foreign remittances, several properties or vehicles, a wealth statement that must reconcile, an AOP or company return, or a notice from FBR. Mistakes on these returns can cost more than the consultant's fee.

How to choose the best tax consultant in Pakistan

Rankings of the "best tax consultant" are usually paid advertising, so judge for yourself. The same five questions work when you compare tax filing companies: the best tax filing company in Pakistan is the one that answers all of them clearly.

  1. Who will actually prepare and review my return, and what are their qualifications?
  2. What is the total fee, and what does it include (registration, return, wealth statement, notices)?
  3. Will I receive a copy of the filed return and the IRIS acknowledgment?
  4. Do you keep my CNIC and IRIS password private, and how do you handle them?
  5. What happens if FBR raises a question after filing?

Be careful with any firm that guarantees a refund or a specific amount of tax saved before seeing your documents.

How much do tax filing services cost?

The government charges nothing to register for an NTN or to submit a return on IRIS. Any fee you pay covers a consultant's preparation and submission work. It varies with the complexity of your return, so ask for the total in writing before you start. Our guide to official fees and consultant fees lists what is free and what is not.

Which tax services do companies and startups need?

A company needs its own NTN after SECP incorporation, an annual return, and often sales tax registration and monthly withholding statements. Read FBR registration after SECP incorporation for the order of steps and corporate tax consultant services in Pakistan for ongoing compliance.

Questions people ask about tax filing services

Is it compulsory to hire a tax consultant to file a return in Pakistan?

No. You can register and file yourself on IRIS at no charge. A consultant is optional, but useful for business income, assets that must reconcile, company returns and notices.

What is the difference between a tax consultant and a tax filing company?

A tax filing company usually sells a standard return service. A tax consultant also advises on planning, notices and audits. Some firms do both, so check what your fee includes.

Can I hire a tax consultant from another city?

Yes. FBR and SECP services are online, so location does not limit the work. You only need to share documents securely and stay reachable.

What documents does a tax consultant need from me?

Usually your CNIC, salary or income details, bank statements, details of property and vehicles, and any FBR notice you received. The exact list depends on your income type.
