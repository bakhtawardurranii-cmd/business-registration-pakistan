Common questions

Taxation services, answered

Is FBR registration the same as SECP company registration?

No. SECP registration creates your company as a legal entity. FBR registration, done afterward through the IRIS portal, gives your company an NTN so it can operate, invoice, and pay taxes.

Do sole proprietors need to register with FBR?

Yes. Sole proprietors register directly with FBR for an NTN since they never file with SECP — this is their only registration step.

When does a business need to register for sales tax?

Generally once a business supplies taxable goods or services above the threshold set by FBR, or if it wants to claim input tax credits.

What happens if I miss the annual tax filing deadline?

Missing the deadline can result in penalties and removal from the Active Taxpayer List (ATL), which raises the withholding tax rate applied to your business on many transactions.
