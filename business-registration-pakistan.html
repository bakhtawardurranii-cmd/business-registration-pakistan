Business registration in Pakistan: which structure should you choose?

Business registration in Pakistan depends on the structure. Private limited companies, single member companies (SMC) and LLPs register with SECP. Partnership firms register with the Registrar of Firms. Sole proprietors register with FBR only. Choose by liability, number of owners and future funding plans.

How do you decide?

  • Want liability protection and you are alone: SMC.
  • Have co-founders or expect investors: private limited company.
  • Professional partners: LLP.
  • Just starting small and accept personal liability: sole proprietorship.

What happens after you register?

Every structure needs tax setup: FBR registration after SECP incorporation. For the registration process itself, see company registration in Pakistan and the DIY guide how to register a company in Pakistan. Overseas founders should read foreign company registration in Pakistan.

Mistakes to avoid when choosing a structure

  • Picking a sole proprietorship, then signing large contracts personally.
  • Forming a private company with a friend without a written shareholders' understanding.
  • Assuming SECP registration also registers you for tax. It does not: see SECP vs FBR: which registration you need.
  • Choosing high share capital without checking how the fee scales: company registration fees in Pakistan.

Questions people ask about business registration

What is the cheapest way to start a business in Pakistan?

A sole proprietorship, which needs only an NTN. It gives no liability protection.

Do I need SECP for a small business?

Not legally for a sole proprietorship. A company structure needs SECP.

Can I change structure later?

Yes. A private company can be converted to an SMC and the reverse under the Companies Act, 2017. Sole proprietors can incorporate later.
