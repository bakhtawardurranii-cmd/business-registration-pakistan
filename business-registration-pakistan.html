Common questions

Business registration, answered

Which company type should I register in Pakistan?

Most single founders register an SMC for limited liability without needing a co-founder. Two or more owners typically choose a private limited company. Professionals such as consultants or agencies sometimes use an LLP instead.

How long does company registration take with SECP?

Name reservation usually clears in 1–2 working days, and full incorporation typically completes in 3–7 working days once your documents are submitted, assuming no objections from SECP.

Do I need a physical office to register a company in Pakistan?

You need a registered address in Pakistan, which can be a home address, a rented office, or a virtual office arrangement — SECP does not require a commercial storefront.

Is an NTN separate from SECP company registration?

Yes. SECP incorporation makes your company a legal entity; you still need to register separately with FBR for an NTN before you can operate, invoice, or file taxes.
