NTN registration in Pakistan: how to get your NTN online

You can complete NTN registration online on FBR's IRIS portal at no government charge. Choose "Registration for Unregistered Person", enter your CNIC, verify the code sent to your NADRA-registered mobile and email, and submit your details. For individuals, your CNIC becomes your NTN.

How to get an NTN number in Pakistan: step by step

  1. Open iris.fbr.gov.pk and choose "Registration for Unregistered Person".
  2. Enter your 13-digit CNIC without dashes and complete the captcha.
  3. Enter the verification codes sent to your mobile number and email.
  4. Complete the registration form with your name, address, occupation or business, and bank details.
  5. Set your password and submit. Individuals receive the NTN immediately. It is your CNIC.
  6. Log in and download your registration as PDF for your records.

If you cannot log in afterwards, see FBR IRIS login and registration help.

NTN registration fee in Pakistan: what does it cost?

FBR charges nothing for NTN registration on IRIS. Any fee you pay a consultant is for preparing documents, submitting the application and fixing errors. It is never a government charge. Firms publish different prices, so compare what each includes. Our fees guide explains what is free and what is not.

Is a company or AOP NTN different from an individual NTN?

Yes. An individual's NTN is their CNIC. A company or AOP is a separate taxpayer and receives its own NTN, even if its owners already have personal ones. A company can register only after SECP incorporation. The order of steps is in FBR registration after SECP incorporation. Sole proprietors do not need a separate NTN; see sole proprietorship registration in Pakistan.

When should you use a consultant instead of registering yourself?

Registering an individual NTN is straightforward when your mobile number is registered in your name. Get help if the CNIC and SIM details do not match, you are registering an AOP or company, you are an overseas Pakistani (overseas Pakistani tax filing and NRP NTN), or you also need sales tax registration (sales tax registration in Pakistan (STRN)).

Questions people ask about NTN registration

Is NTN registration free in Pakistan?

Yes. FBR does not charge for NTN registration through IRIS. Only consultants charge a service fee.

Is my CNIC my NTN?

For individuals, yes. Companies and AOPs receive a separate NTN.

How long does NTN registration take?

For individuals with correct CNIC and mobile details it is usually immediate. Companies and AOPs may take longer if documents need correction.

Can I get an NTN without a business?

Yes. Salaried people, landlords and investors can register. An NTN is what allows you to file a return and become a filer.
