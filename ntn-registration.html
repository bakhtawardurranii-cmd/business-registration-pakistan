Common questions

NTN registration, answered

What is an NTN?

An NTN, or National Tax Number, is the identifier FBR assigns to individuals and companies for tax purposes. It's required before you can invoice clients, open a business bank account, or file any tax return.

How long does NTN registration take?

Typically same-day to within 3 working days once your documents are submitted correctly through FBR's IRIS portal.

Do I need a separate NTN for my company and myself?

Yes. Individuals have a personal NTN tied to their CNIC, and a SECP-registered company needs its own separate company NTN, even if you already have a personal one.
