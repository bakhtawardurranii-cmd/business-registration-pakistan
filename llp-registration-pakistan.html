LLP registration in Pakistan: limited liability for partners

A limited liability partnership (LLP) is registered with SECP and gives its partners limited liability while keeping a partnership-style structure. It suits professionals such as consultants, accountants and agencies who want to share ownership without the shareholder and director layers of a company.

How does an LLP differ from a partnership firm?

A partnership firm registers with the Registrar of Firms and its partners are personally liable without limit. An LLP registers with SECP and limits partners' liability.

LLP or private limited company?

A private limited company uses shares and directors and is better suited to raising investment. An LLP uses a partnership agreement and suits professional firms. For a full comparison, read business registration in Pakistan.

What do you need to register an LLP?

Partners' CNICs, a proposed name, an LLP agreement and a registered address. Confirm current SECP requirements for LLPs before filing.

What should an LLP agreement settle before you register?

  • Each partner's capital contribution and profit share.
  • Who can sign contracts and spend money.
  • How decisions are made and how disputes are resolved.
  • What happens when a partner leaves or dies.

Settling these on paper is cheaper than settling them in a dispute. Compare structures again at business registration in Pakistan if you are unsure.

Questions people ask about an LLP

Is an LLP registered with SECP?

Yes.

Do LLP partners have limited liability?

Yes, unlike partners in an ordinary partnership firm.

Who should pick an LLP?

Professional service firms with two or more partners.
