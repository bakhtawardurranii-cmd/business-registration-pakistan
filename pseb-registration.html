PSEB registration for IT & software exporters.

PSEB (Pakistan Software Export Board) registration is required for IT and software exporting companies to access incentives like income tax exemptions on export revenue and smoother foreign exchange handling.

Why it's worth it

What PSEB registration unlocks

PSEB registration is a common next step once an IT or software company has incorporated with SECP and registered for an NTN — it's specifically about accessing export-related incentives, not a replacement for either of those steps.

Registered exporters can access reduced or exempted income tax on qualifying export revenue and simplified processes for receiving payments from overseas clients, among other benefits.

Common questions

Answered

Who needs to register with PSEB?

IT and software export companies typically register with the Pakistan Software Export Board to access export incentives, including income tax exemptions on IT export revenue and streamlined foreign exchange handling for export proceeds.

Is PSEB registration required before or after SECP incorporation?

After. You need an incorporated company (or registered sole proprietorship/partnership) with SECP or FBR before you can register with PSEB.

Does PSEB registration replace FBR tax registration?

No, PSEB registration is separate from and in addition to your NTN and any other FBR registrations — it specifically unlocks IT-sector export incentives.
