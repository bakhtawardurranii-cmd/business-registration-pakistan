About Business Registration Pakistan

Business Registration Pakistan is an independent consultancy in Karachi helping people and businesses with NTN and tax filing, SECP company registration and trademark filing. We are not part of SECP, FBR or IPO-Pakistan. This site pairs free guides with optional professional help.

Ask us on WhatsAppSend an enquiry

What do we do?

We cover three areas: tax filing services, company registration and trademark registration. You can use the guides to do the work yourself, or contact us to have it handled.

How are the guides written and kept accurate?

Each guide starts with the reader's problem and answers it directly. Facts about deadlines, slab rates, SECP rules and trademark procedure were checked against several published sources when the page was written, and each page shows its last update date. Where sources disagree, for example on SECP fee tiers and non-filer rates, we say so instead of printing a single figure. Rules change with every Finance Act, so confirm important figures on the official sites: iris.fbr.gov.pk, secp.gov.pk and ipo.gov.pk.
