Why we exist

Most founders end up juggling three different consultants

Registering a company is one conversation. Protecting the brand name is a different one. Staying compliant with FBR every year is a third. Most businesses end up with three separate relationships for three things that are, in practice, deeply connected — your NTN depends on your SECP certificate, and your trademark strategy often follows directly from how you've structured the company.

We built this consultancy to cover all three under one roof, filing directly with SECP, IPO-Pakistan, and FBR ourselves rather than routing your paperwork through intermediaries. One point of contact, one place that already has your documents on file, whichever of the three you need next.
