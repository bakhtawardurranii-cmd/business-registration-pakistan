How to register a trademark in Pakistan, step by step

To register a trademark in Pakistan, search the register, choose your class, file the application with IPO-Pakistan, answer any examination objections, wait through publication and the two-month opposition period, then pay the registration fee to receive the certificate.

Step 1: Trademark search in Pakistan before you file

A search is not compulsory but it saves months. Check IPO-Pakistan's records for identical or similar marks in your class. A search covers only what is already on record, not marks filed this week.

Step 2: Choose the right class

Each application covers one class of goods or services. Choose carefully: trademark classes in Pakistan (Nice classification).

Step 3: File the application

File the application on the prescribed form with the filing fee (see trademark registration cost in Pakistan). A power of attorney is needed if an agent files for you.

Step 4: Examination and objections

The registrar examines the mark, for example whether it is descriptive, too similar to an existing mark, or lacks distinctiveness. If objections are raised you must reply in writing within the time allowed, or the application can be abandoned.

Step 5: Publication and the two-month opposition period

Accepted marks are published in the Trade Marks Journal. Anyone can oppose within two months, extendable on request. If someone opposes, you file a counterstatement and the registrar decides.

Step 6: Registration and renewal

Pay the registration fee after acceptance to receive the certificate. Protection runs ten years from filing. Renew in time: trademark renewal and infringement in Pakistan.

Questions people ask about the trademark filing steps

How long does the whole process take?

Often 12 to 18 months when unopposed, longer with objections or oppositions.

Can I file the trademark myself?

Yes, but examination replies and oppositions are easier with a professional.

Do I need a company to file?

No. Individuals can file. See trademark registration for choices.
