How to register a trademark in Pakistan: the complete guide.

Registering a trademark in Pakistan means searching for availability, filing with IPO-Pakistan under the correct class, and clearing a four-month public opposition period. Filing takes days; full registration typically takes 12–18 months.

The full process

Six steps from search to certificate

1

Search for availability

We check IPO-Pakistan's database to confirm your mark isn't already registered in your class.

2

Choose your class

Trademarks are filed under one of 45 Nice Classification classes based on your goods/services.

3

File your application

We submit your application to IPO-Pakistan with the required forms and fee.

4

Examination & publication

IPO-Pakistan examines the mark and publishes it in the Trademark Journal.

5

Opposition period

A four-month window during which third parties can formally object.

6

Get your certificate

If unopposed, you receive a registration certificate valid for 10 years, renewable.

Good to know

Common mistakes founders make

Filing after launch

Pakistan is first-to-file — waiting too long risks someone else registering your name first.

Wrong class

Filing under the wrong Nice class means your trademark won't actually cover your real business activity.
