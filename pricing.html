Tax filing charges in Pakistan: what is free and what you pay for

FBR charges nothing to register an NTN or to file a return on IRIS. SECP and IPO-Pakistan do charge government fees, which depend on capital and class. Anything beyond that is a consultant's fee for preparation and follow-up. Ask any provider for the total in writing first.

What does a consultant's fee cover?

  • Preparing and checking documents.
  • Submitting and following up.
  • Fixing errors and responding to queries.
  • For returns, reconciling income, wealth and withholding.

It is never a government charge. Read how we handle documents in our privacy policy.

How to compare tax filing charges between providers

  1. Ask what the fee includes: registration, return, wealth statement, revisions.
  2. Ask about extra charges for notices or late items.
  3. Ask for the filed return and acknowledgment.
  4. Be careful with prices far below the rest of the market.

We quote after understanding your case; contact us with your situation using the contact form.

Red flags in tax filing offers

  • Guaranteed refunds before your documents are seen.
  • Requests for your IRIS password and the verification codes on your phone.
  • Prices with no written scope.
  • No copy of the filed return or acknowledgment.
