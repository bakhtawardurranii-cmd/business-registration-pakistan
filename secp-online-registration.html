Answered

What's the difference between eZfile and LEAP?

eZfile is SECP's original online filing system; LEAP is the newer platform SECP has been rolling out to eventually replace it, with a more modern interface and expanded services.

Do I need a digital signature to file online with SECP?

Yes, most SECP online filings require a digital signature to legally authenticate the submission, which is separate from your regular signature.

Can I complete the entire registration process online?

Yes, from name reservation through to receiving your certificate of incorporation, SECP's online portals allow the entire process to be completed without visiting a physical office.
