Naming rules

What SECP checks before approving a name

Your proposed name can't be identical, or confusingly similar, to any company already on SECP's register. It also can't use certain restricted words — like "Bank," "Insurance," "Foundation," or "Chamber of Commerce" — without separate regulatory approval to back it up.

We run the check against SECP's database before you commit, and suggest alternates upfront if your first choice looks likely to be rejected, so you don't lose time waiting on a name that was never going to clear.

Common questions

Answered

Why does SECP reject some company names?

SECP rejects names that are identical or deceptively similar to an existing registered company, or that use restricted words (like 'Bank', 'Insurance', or 'Foundation') without the required additional approval.

How long is a reserved name valid for?

Once SECP approves your name reservation, it's typically held for a limited window (commonly 60 days) during which you must complete incorporation, after which the reservation can lapse.

Can I reserve more than one name at a time?

Yes, SECP allows you to submit alternate name choices in case your first preference is rejected, speeding up the overall process.
