Filer vs non-filer in Pakistan: what really changes when you are not on the ATL

A filer is someone on FBR's Active Taxpayer List (ATL). A non-filer is not. The difference shows up as higher tax collected from you when you sell property, take profit on savings, register a vehicle or make other withholding payments. Filing your return is what moves you onto the list.

Is a filer the same as someone with an NTN?

No, and this catches many people out. An NTN only means you are registered. You become a filer when you file your return for the tax year and appear on the ATL. You can hold an NTN for years and still be treated as a non-filer. You can check where you stand in two minutes with the ATL check by CNIC.

Did the late filer category go away?

For property, tax year 2026 had a middle tier for late filers. Professional commentary on the Finance Act 2026 says that tier has been removed for tax year 2027, so for property you are either on the ATL or you are not. Because that comes from commentary on a new law, check the property rate card on FBR's website before you sign anything.

What about overseas Pakistanis?

FBR's published FAQ says an overseas Pakistani who holds a POC or NICOP and is non-resident (in Pakistan less than 183 days in the financial year) can pay property advance tax at the filer rate even if they are not on the ATL. The route is through the property transfer process, with documents uploaded. The details are in overseas Pakistani tax filing and NTN.

How do you move from non-filer to filer?

  1. Get an NTN if you do not have one (NTN registration in Pakistan).
  2. File your income tax return for the tax year, with your wealth statement if it applies. Here is what you need to gather.
  3. Pay any surcharge that applies if you filed after the due date.
  4. Wait for the ATL update, then check your status. Keep your filing acknowledgment until you appear.

The full route, with the usual stumbling points, is in how to become a tax filer in Pakistan.

Questions people ask about filer vs non-filer

Do non-filers pay the same salary tax?

The salary tax slabs are the same. The extra cost for non-filers comes from higher withholding on other transactions such as property and bank profit.

Can I become a filer if I have no income?

You may not be required to file below the taxable threshold, but many people file anyway to stay on the ATL and avoid higher withholding. Ask before you decide, because it depends on your situation.

How long does it take to appear on the ATL after filing?

FBR updates the list periodically, so it can take some time. Keep the acknowledgment from your return in case you need to show it.

Is there a difference between NTN holder and filer?

Yes. An NTN is a registration. Filer status comes from filing your return and appearing on the ATL.

Where can I see the exact non-filer rates?

On FBR's withholding tax rate cards. We summarise them in our non-filer tax rates guide, but the rate card is the final word.
