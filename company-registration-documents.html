Common questions

Answered

Do document requirements differ by company type?

Yes. A private limited company or SMC needs MOA/AOA and CNIC copies of all directors/shareholders, while a sole proprietorship only needs your own CNIC and proof of address to register with FBR.

Can I use a virtual office address as my registered address?

Yes, SECP accepts a virtual office arrangement as a registered address, as long as you can produce proof of the arrangement if asked.

Do foreign directors need any additional documents?

Foreign nationals typically provide a passport copy instead of a CNIC, and may need additional documentation depending on the sector and shareholding structure.
