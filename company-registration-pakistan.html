What it means

Registering a company gives your business its own legal identity

Once SECP approves your incorporation, your company can own assets, sign contracts, hire staff, and be sued or sue in its own name — separate from you personally. That separation is what limits your personal liability if the business runs into debt or legal trouble.

Sole proprietorships skip SECP entirely and register directly with FBR instead — there's no separate legal entity, so the owner is personally liable for everything. See if that's a better fit for you →

Registering also unlocks things a sole proprietorship can't: opening a corporate bank account, applying for larger contracts and tenders that require a registered entity, and — if you ever plan to raise investment — issuing shares to investors.

Common questions

Company registration, answered

What is company registration in Pakistan?

Company registration is the process of legally incorporating a business with SECP, most commonly as a private limited company or SMC, which gives the business its own legal identity separate from its owners.

Is company registration done through SECP or FBR?

A company is incorporated through SECP. FBR registration for an NTN and tax filing happens separately, afterward, once your SECP incorporation certificate is issued.

Can a foreigner or overseas Pakistani register a company in Pakistan?

Yes. Foreign nationals and overseas Pakistanis can register a company in Pakistan, though foreign shareholding may require additional filings with the Board of Investment depending on the sector.

Is there a minimum capital requirement to register a company?

For most private limited companies and SMCs there is no mandatory minimum paid-up capital, though certain regulated sectors do set their own minimums.
