Company registration in Pakistan: what it involves and how to get it done

Company registration in Pakistan means incorporating with SECP under the Companies Act, 2017. You reserve a name, file your memorandum and articles with the required documents, pay the SECP fee and receive a certificate of incorporation. Then you register with FBR for an NTN.

What do you get from SECP company registration in Pakistan?

A separate legal entity that can own assets, sign contracts, open bank accounts and limit its owners' liability. Most founders choose a private limited company or an SMC.

Which structure should you choose?

Most people comparing structures are really choosing between four options: registering a company with SECP, or staying unregistered with FBR only as a sole proprietor or partnership. The table below is a starting point; each linked guide covers the detail.

If you plan to raise outside investment, hire formally, sign contracts as a company, or simply want the owner's personal assets separated from business debts, a private limited company or SMC is usually the better fit over a sole proprietorship or plain partnership.

What is the process for registering a company with SECP?

  1. Choose the structure.
  2. Reserve a name. SECP reserves it for a period not exceeding sixty days.
  3. Prepare the MOA, AOA and director and member details.
  4. File through SECP's eServices portal (see SECP online registration and digital signature) and pay the fee.
  5. Receive the certificate of incorporation.
  6. Register with FBR and open a bank account.

What documents do you need?

  • CNICs (or passports for foreign directors) of all directors and subscribers.
  • Three proposed company names, in order of preference, for the name-availability check.
  • A registered office address in Pakistan.
  • Memorandum and Articles of Association (MOA and AOA), or SECP's standard-form versions for a simple private limited company.
  • Details of share capital and how it is divided among subscribers.
  • Contact details (mobile and email) for each director, since SECP verifies these during filing.

How long does SECP registration take?

It depends on how complete the documents are and on any queries from SECP. Name reservation and incorporation can be filed together. SECP's own materials describe some straightforward, complete filings being confirmed within about a week through eServices, and name-availability decisions typically come back within a couple of working days. If SECP raises an objection, you get an email, correct the issue and resubmit through the same filing rather than starting over. Promises of an exact number of days should be treated with caution.

Worked example: registering a two-director private limited company

Two founders in Karachi want to incorporate a private limited company with Rs 100,000 in share capital, split evenly.

  1. They shortlist three possible names and run a name-availability search on SECP's eServices portal.
  2. One name is approved within a couple of working days; SECP reserves it for up to sixty days.
  3. They prepare the MOA and AOA (using SECP's standard templates for a simple case), list themselves as directors and subscribers, and confirm the registered office address.
  4. They file the incorporation application online, upload CNICs and the MOA/AOA, and pay the SECP fee for the Rs 100,000 capital tier.
  5. SECP reviews the filing. If nothing is queried, the certificate of incorporation and CUIN (Company Universal Identification Number) are issued electronically.
  6. With the certificate in hand, they register for an NTN on IRIS (free) and open a company bank account, which the bank will ask to see the certificate and MOA/AOA for.

Where this commonly slows down: a proposed name is too similar to an existing one, a director's CNIC details do not match NADRA's records, or the MOA's stated business objects are too vague. Each of these triggers an objection and a resubmission rather than an outright rejection.

Why do applications get objected to or delayed?

  • Name too similar to an existing company or trademark. Run a name search first, and consider checking trademark registration too, since a clear company name search does not guarantee the name is free to trademark.
  • Mismatched CNIC or contact details. Names, addresses or dates that do not match NADRA's or IRIS's records commonly trigger a query.
  • Vague or overly broad objects clause in the MOA, which SECP may ask you to narrow or clarify.
  • Incomplete subscriber or director information, such as a missing signature, photograph or nominee for an SMC.
  • Registered office address that cannot be verified, such as a P.O. box or an address with no supporting document.

Preparing documents completely the first time is the single biggest lever you have over how long registration takes.

Questions people ask about company registration

Do I need an office to register a company?

You need a registered office address in Pakistan. It must be a real address where you can receive official mail.

Is there a minimum capital?

The Companies Act, 2017 does not impose a mandatory minimum paid-up capital for most private companies.

Can a foreigner register a company?

Yes. See foreign company registration.

What happens if my company name is rejected?

You are notified by email and can submit an alternative name from your shortlist, or a new one, without restarting the whole filing.

Do I need a lawyer to draft the MOA and AOA?

Not for a simple company using SECP's standard-form MOA and AOA. Custom objects clauses, multiple share classes or unusual shareholder arrangements are where legal drafting help is worth it.
