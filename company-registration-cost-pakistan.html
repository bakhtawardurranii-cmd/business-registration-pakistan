What company registration actually costs in Pakistan.

SECP charges a government filing fee based on your company's share capital, separate from any service fee you pay a consultant. As of SECP’s most recent fee revision, electronic filing starts at roughly PKR 11,000 for share capital up to PKR 100,000.

Common questions

Registration cost, answered

How much does it cost to register a company in Pakistan?

SECP's own electronic filing fee is roughly PKR 11,000 for a company with share capital up to PKR 100,000, plus a service fee if you use a consultant. Total cost scales with your declared share capital.

Does the SECP fee depend on share capital?

Yes. SECP charges a base fee for the first slab of nominal share capital, then an additional incremental fee for every further slab up to PKR 10 million.

Are SECP fees the same for electronic and manual filing?

No. SECP charges a lower fee for documents filed electronically through eZfile/LEAP than for physical, manual submission.
