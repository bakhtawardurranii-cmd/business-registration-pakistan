Sales tax on services in Islamabad

Islamabad is federal territory, not a province, so sales tax on services here is not handled by PRA, SRB, KPRA or BRA. It is collected by FBR itself, under the Islamabad Capital Territory (Tax on Services) Ordinance, 2001. Restaurants, hotels, IT and consultancy services and several other categories in the capital register and file through this ordinance rather than a provincial authority. See provincial sales tax on services for how this compares with Punjab, Sindh and KP, and sales tax registration in Pakistan for the federal goods side.
