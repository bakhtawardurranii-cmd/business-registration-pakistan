Income tax filing in Lahore

Filing is online, so the process is the same as anywhere in Pakistan. See income tax filing in Pakistan. Lahore clients can bring documents in person if they prefer.

NTN registration in Lahore

NTN registration is free on IRIS. If your details do not match or you are registering a company or AOP, we can help in person or online: NTN registration in Pakistan.

PRA registration in Lahore

Service businesses in Punjab, including restaurants, IT firms and consultants, deal with the Punjab Revenue Authority for sales tax on services. See provincial sales tax on services (PRA, SRB, KPRA, BRA).
