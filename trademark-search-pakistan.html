Why search first

What a search catches before you file

Pakistan is a first-to-file jurisdiction — whoever files first generally wins the trademark, regardless of who used the name first in practice. A search tells you whether someone has already filed something identical or confusingly similar in your class, before you spend months waiting through examination and opposition.

It's not a guarantee — a search only covers what's already in IPO-Pakistan's database, not marks filed the same week. But it's the single best way to avoid an expensive rejection after you've already committed to a brand name.

Common questions

Answered

Is a trademark search mandatory before filing in Pakistan?

No, a pre-filing search isn't mandatory, but skipping it means filing blind — you risk rejection or a costly dispute later if an identical or similar mark already exists.

What can a trademark search tell me?

A search checks IPO-Pakistan's database for identical or similar marks already registered or pending in your class, giving you an early signal on whether your name or logo is likely to clear.

How long does a trademark search take?

A search typically takes a few working days to complete and report back on.
