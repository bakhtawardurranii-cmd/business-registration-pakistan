FBR IRIS login help: log in, register and reset your password

To use FBR IRIS login, go to iris.fbr.gov.pk, choose Taxpayer Login and enter your CNIC without dashes (individuals) or your NTN (companies and AOPs) with your password. If you forgot the password, use the reset option and verify with the mobile number and email registered on IRIS.

How do you log in to FBR IRIS?

  1. Open the official portal, iris.fbr.gov.pk.
  2. Select Taxpayer Login.
  3. Enter your registration number: CNIC without dashes for individuals, NTN for companies and AOPs.
  4. Enter your password and the captcha, then sign in.

Type the address yourself or use a bookmark. Do not log in from links in messages or ads.

IRIS FBR password reset: how do you reset a forgotten password?

Use the forgot-password option on the login page. FBR sends a verification code to the mobile number and email stored on your IRIS profile. If you no longer have access to either, you may need to update your contact details with FBR first, which is where a consultant can help. Never share verification codes with anyone.

IRIS FBR registration: how do you create an account?

A new user registers through "Registration for Unregistered Person" on the same site. The full process, documents and fees are in NTN registration in Pakistan. Registration is free.

Why can't you log in? Common causes

  • CNIC typed with dashes or with a typing error.
  • Password expired or entered with the wrong case.
  • Verification code sent to an old mobile number.
  • Browser blocking pop-ups or cookies for the site.
  • Portal congestion near the deadline; try early in the morning. See the return deadline.

Keeping your IRIS account safe

Your IRIS account can file returns and change bank details. Use a strong, unique password, keep your mobile number current, and be careful about sharing access. If you give a consultant your login, change the password afterwards. Once you are in, follow income tax filing in Pakistan.

Questions people ask about IRIS login

What is the official IRIS website?

iris.fbr.gov.pk is FBR's official portal for registration, returns, wealth statements and notices.

What is my IRIS username?

For individuals it is the CNIC without dashes. Companies and AOPs use their NTN.

Is IRIS free to use?

Yes. FBR does not charge for registration or return filing on IRIS.

Can a consultant reset my IRIS password for me?

They can guide you, but the verification codes go to your registered mobile and email, so you will still need to be involved.
