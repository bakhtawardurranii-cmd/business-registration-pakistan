FBR Registration in Pakistan

Registering with the Federal Board of Revenue comes before any tax return. We set up your e-enrolment on the IRIS portal — as an individual, a partnership or a company — and coordinate any in-person Tax House step.

What's Included

Get registered correctly the first time.

FBR registration looks different depending on who you are. The documents, the route and the number you receive all change with your registration type.

✓

Registration-type review

We confirm whether you should register as an individual, an association of persons (AOP) or a company, so the registration matches how you actually earn.

✓

IRIS e-enrolment

We complete your registration on FBR's IRIS portal, which is the system that treats you as a registered taxpayer once you are e-enrolled.

✓

Document check before submission

Your CNIC, mobile number, bank certificate and business-premises papers are checked against FBR's published list before anything is filed.

✓

Tax House coordination

For AOPs and companies, we prepare the authorisation letter and paperwork for the authorised person's visit to the Tax House facilitation counter.

✓

Business details entered correctly

Business name, address, principal activity and accounting period are set up accurately, so they don't need correcting later.

✓

Handover and next steps

You receive your registration number and IRIS access, plus a clear explanation of what comes next: filing your return and reaching the Active Taxpayer List.

Which route applies?

Individual registration vs. AOP or company registration.

The online route is simplest for individuals. Partnerships and companies register on the same portal, but the process usually includes an in-person step.

Fully online

Individual or Sole Proprietor

Registered on IRIS with your own CNIC
  • WhoSalaried individuals, freelancers and sole proprietors.
  • RouteOnline e-enrolment through the IRIS portal.
  • Your numberYour 13-digit CNIC is used as your NTN once you are e-enrolled.
  • Key papersCNIC, personal mobile SIM and email, a bank account certificate, and premises papers if you run a business.

AOP or Company

IRIS registration plus a Tax House visit
  • WhoPartnerships and other AOPs, private limited and other companies.
  • RouteE-enrolment on IRIS, with one authorised person attending a Tax House facilitation counter.
  • Your numberA separate 7-digit NTN is issued after e-enrolment.
  • Key papersPartnership deed or incorporation certificate, CNICs of all partners or directors, and a signed authorisation letter.
How It Works

From documents to a registered taxpayer.

The steps are the same for everyone. What changes is the paperwork behind each one.

1

Confirm your type

We identify whether you register as an individual, AOP or company, and what income you'll be reporting.

2

Gather and check documents

You share your papers and we check them against FBR's current requirements before submission.

3

Enrol on IRIS

Your registration is submitted on the IRIS portal, with the Tax House step arranged for AOPs and companies.

4

Confirm and hand over

We verify the registration is active, give you your access details, and explain your first filing date.

What You'll Need

Documents to have ready.

Based on FBR's published requirements. Exact needs vary by case, and we confirm them with you before we file.

✓
Valid CNIC (for an AOP or company, the CNICs of all partners or directors)
✓
Mobile number with a SIM registered against your own CNIC
✓
Personal email address (for an AOP or company, an email belonging to the business)
✓
Bank account maintenance certificate, in your name or in the name of the AOP or company
✓
If you have a business: tenancy or ownership evidence for the premises, plus a paid utility bill not older than 3 months
✓
AOP: partnership deed and Registrar of Firms certificate. Company: certificate of incorporation
✓
AOP or company: an authorisation letter signed by all partners or directors, naming the person who will register
