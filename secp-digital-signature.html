Getting a digital signature for SECP filing.

A digital signature is required to file documents electronically through SECP’s eZfile or LEAP portals, serving as your legally recognized electronic signature for company registration and ongoing filings.
Get help obtaining one See the online filing process

What it's for

Where a digital signature is required

Beyond initial company incorporation, a digital signature is used for ongoing SECP filings — annual returns, changes in directorship, and other statutory updates submitted through the online portal.

We help coordinate obtaining a digital signature as part of your registration package, so you're not left figuring out a separate process after your certificate of incorporation is already issued.

Common questions

Answered

Why do I need a digital signature for SECP filings?

SECP's online portals (eZfile and LEAP) require a digital signature to legally authenticate filings submitted electronically, serving the same legal function as a wet-ink signature on paper documents.

Who issues digital signatures for SECP filings?

Digital signatures used for SECP filings are typically issued by SECP-approved certification service providers.

Does every director need their own digital signature?

Typically, at least one authorized signatory needs a digital signature to submit filings, though requirements can vary depending on the specific filing type.
