Become a filer in Pakistan: NTN, return and ATL in three steps

To become a filer in Pakistan, register for an NTN on IRIS, file your income tax return for the tax year, and pay any tax or surcharge due. Once FBR lists you on the Active Taxpayer List, you pay lower withholding tax on many bank, property and vehicle transactions.

How to become a tax filer in Pakistan: the three steps

  1. Register. Get your NTN free on IRIS: NTN registration in Pakistan.
  2. File. Submit the return for the tax year, even if income is low: income tax filing in Pakistan.
  3. Confirm. Check you appear on the list: Active Taxpayer List (ATL) check by CNIC.

Filer vs non-filer in Pakistan: what is the difference?

A filer has filed the required return and appears on the ATL. A non-filer either never registered or did not file. The law applies higher withholding rates to persons not on the ATL, on transactions such as bank profit, cash withdrawals, property purchases and sales, and vehicle registration. Exact rates change with each Finance Act and published figures disagree, so see non-filer tax rates in Pakistan and FBR's current rate card.

What are the benefits of being a filer in Pakistan?

  • Lower withholding tax on many everyday transactions.
  • Ability to buy and sell property and register vehicles with less tax deducted.
  • A tax record that banks, embassies and tenders often ask for.
  • Ability to claim refunds of excess tax withheld.

Do you have to pay tax to become a filer?

No. Filing a return is what makes you a filer. If your income is below the taxable threshold you may owe nothing. The obligation is to declare correctly.

Can you become a filer after the deadline?

Yes. File the return as soon as you can. Late filers may face a penalty or surcharge and a delay before appearing on the list. See the deadline guide.

Roman Urdu: filer kaise bane?

Filer banne ke liye pehle NTN banwayen, phir apna income tax return file karein, aur ATL mein naam aane ka intezar karein. Return file karna hi aapko filer banata hai.

Questions people ask about becoming a filer

How long does it take to become a filer?

Once you file, FBR includes you in the next ATL update. The timing depends on when FBR publishes it.

Can a housewife or student become a filer?

Yes, anyone with an NTN can file a return. Declare any income, assets and expenses honestly.

Do I need a consultant to become a filer?

Not always. If you have salary only, you can do it yourself. If you have property, business income or foreign income, professional help avoids errors.
