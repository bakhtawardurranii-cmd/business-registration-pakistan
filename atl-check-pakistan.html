Active Taxpayer List check: how to see if you are a filer

To do an active taxpayer list check, look up your CNIC or NTN on FBR's ATL verification page, or use the SMS service FBR publishes for ATL status. If your name appears, you are a filer. If not, you are treated as a non-filer until you file your return.

How do you check filer status by CNIC?

  1. Use FBR's official ATL and online verification pages, reached through iris.fbr.gov.pk.
  2. Enter your 13-digit CNIC without dashes. Companies and AOPs enter their NTN.
  3. Read the result: Active means you are on the list.

FBR has also published an SMS method, which is to send ATL followed by your CNIC to 9966. Confirm the current method on FBR's site, since services change. We do not run a live lookup on this website, so you always see FBR's own answer.

NTN verification in Pakistan: online NTN verification by CNIC

Use FBR's online verification with the CNIC or NTN. It shows whether the number is registered and to whom, which is useful before you trade with a business. Registration status and filer status are different: an NTN can exist while the holder is not on the ATL.

What does the result mean?

ATL results explained
ResultMeaningNext step
ActiveYou filed the required return and are on the list.Keep filing on time each year.
Inactive or not foundYou are registered but not on the list, or not registered at all.Register if needed, then file. See below.

Not on the ATL: how do you get on it?

Register for an NTN if you have none (NTN registration in Pakistan), file the return for the relevant tax year (income tax filing in Pakistan), and pay any surcharge the law requires for late filers. The full route is in how to become a tax filer in Pakistan. Updates to the list are not instant, so keep your filing acknowledgment as proof.

Questions people ask about the ATL check

Is the ATL check free?

Yes. FBR does not charge to check ATL status.

Why am I a non-filer when I have an NTN?

Because filer status depends on filing your return, not only on registering.

How often is the ATL updated?

FBR updates it periodically. Keep your filing acknowledgment in case the list has not yet caught up.
