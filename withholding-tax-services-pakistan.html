Common triggers

Where withholding tax usually applies

Salaries

Employers withhold income tax from employee salaries based on applicable slab rates.

Contractor & vendor payments

Payments to contractors and suppliers often carry a withholding obligation at source.

Utility & bank transactions

Certain utility bills and banking transactions carry their own withholding tax treatment.

Common questions

Answered

What is withholding tax?

Withholding tax is tax deducted at source on certain payments — such as salaries, contractor payments, and utility bills — by the payer, who then deposits it with FBR on the recipient's behalf.

Who is required to withhold tax?

Companies and certain other entities designated as withholding agents are required to deduct tax at the applicable rate when making specified payments, and to file periodic withholding statements.

What happens if a business fails to withhold tax correctly?

Failing to withhold or deposit tax correctly can expose the withholding agent to penalties and the liability for the tax that should have been deducted.
